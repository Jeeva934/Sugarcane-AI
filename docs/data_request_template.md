### 1. Research Context & Purpose
We are developing an open-source, non-commercial decision support system: **"Explainable AI-Based Multi-Modal Decision Support System for Sugarcane Disease Risk Assessment and Diagnosis"**. The objective is to provide smallholder sugarcane cultivators with early epidemiological risk warnings (7-day to 14-day forecast window) to prevent catastrophic outbreaks of wilt (*Cephalosporium sacchari* / *Fusarium sacchari*), red rot (*Colletotrichum falcatum*), and rust (*Puccinia melanocephala*).

While satellite reanalysis and gridded weather data (Open-Meteo / ERA5) can provide regional estimates, empirical calibration against ground-truth field disease incidence is critical to establishing defensible, scientifically validated epidemiological thresholds.

### 2. Specific Data Requested
If permissible under institutional data-sharing guidelines, we would be deeply grateful for:
1. **Dated Disease Incidence Records:** Fortnightly or monthly disease incidence percentages ($DI\%$) or disease severity indices for sugarcane wilt and red rot across experimental plots at SRI Pusa (or collaborative AICRP centers) for the available growing seasons (e.g., 2018–2024).
2. **Matching Meteorological Station Logs:** Parallel daily or standard meteorological week (SMW) observations from the Pusa agrometeorological observatory:
   - Maximum & Minimum Temperature (°C)
   - Morning (07:00 hrs) & Evening (14:00 hrs) Relative Humidity (%)
   - Daily Rainfall (mm) & Rainy Days count
   - Bright Sunshine Hours (BSS, hrs/day) and Wind Velocity (km/h)
3. **Crop Phenology & Varietal Context:** Cultivar susceptibility classifications (e.g., CoP 2061, CoP 9301, BO 91) and planting/ratooning dates where recorded.

### 3. Data Governance & Academic Commitments

- **Strict Non-Commercial Use:** The data will be used exclusively for non-profit academic research, scientific validation, and educational open-source modeling.

- **Collaborative Co-Authorship:** If desired by the department, we warmly invite your team to collaborate and co-author resulting publications detailing the epidemiological validation of the machine learning risk models.
- **Security & Non-Redistribution:** Raw data tables will be securely stored and will not be redistributed or released to third parties without prior written authorization.
